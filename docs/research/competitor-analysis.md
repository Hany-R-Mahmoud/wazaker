# Competitor Analysis

## Reference Product

Tasmee / تسميع

## Observed Capability Groups

### Core

- mushaf reading
- AI recitation checking
- memorization support
- khatmah planning

### Support

- settings and personalization
- reciter playback
- bookmarks and reading controls

### Expansion

- azkar
- prayer times
- qibla
- competitions
- commerce

## Product Insight

The strongest product opportunity is not copying the super-app breadth. It is building a more trustworthy and clearer recitation revision flow.

## Open Research Questions

- how does Tasmee handle low-confidence results
- how accurate is its comparison on imperfect recitation
- what do reviews complain about most
- which features actually drive retention versus app-store marketing
