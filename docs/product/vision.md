# Product Vision

## Working Name

wazaker

## One-Sentence Goal

Build a free, Arabic-first mobile app for Quran recitation practice and memorization, with trustworthy AI-assisted feedback.

## Target User

Primary Phase 1 target:

- Arabic-speaking Quran learners and memorizers
- early cohort of up to 100 users
- users who want guided self-practice without waiting for a teacher to be present

## Primary Promise

The app helps a learner open the Quran, listen to an ayah recited correctly, record their own recitation, and receive confidence-aware feedback that is useful without overstating certainty.

## Why This Product

Most Islamic apps try to become broad utility bundles. `wazaker` is intentionally narrower:

- Quran-first
- practice-first
- trust-first
- Arabic-first

The product direction is inspired by the usefulness of Tarteel’s focused recitation experience, but it is intentionally scoped to a smaller, more controlled launch with strong privacy and accuracy constraints.

## Product Principles

- Trust first: never present uncertain output as certain correction.
- Arabic first: Arabic is the default learner experience; English is support, not the center.
- Privacy by architecture: user recitation audio stays on the controlled VPS path.
- Focus over breadth: do not drift into super-app features.
- Learn by repetition: optimize for short, repeatable ayah-by-ayah practice loops.
- Build on evidence: ASR quality must be validated before stronger claims are exposed.
