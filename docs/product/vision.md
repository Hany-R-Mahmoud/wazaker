# Product Vision

## Working Name

wazaker

## One-Sentence Goal

Build a mobile app that helps Quran learners revise and assess recitation with AI support.

## Target User

Quran learners who want guided revision while reciting from memory.

## Primary Promise

The app helps a learner read Quran and revise memorized passages by listening to recitation, comparing it against the expected text, and returning simple, trustworthy feedback.

## Why This Product

Most Islamic utility apps bundle many unrelated features. The strongest opportunity is a focused revision coach that solves one important problem well: helping a learner know whether they recited correctly.

## Product Principles

- Trust first: never pretend certainty when confidence is low.
- Focus over breadth: protect the recitation workflow from utility-app sprawl.
- Mobile-first: optimize for short, repeatable daily sessions.
- Clear feedback: show mistakes in a way a learner can act on immediately.
- Bilingual support: Arabic-first, English-supported for navigation and settings.
