# Skill: standup
# Version: 1.0
# Model: llama-3.1-8b
# Last updated: 2026-03-22

## Purpose

Produce concise daily standup summaries from commits, Plane tasks, and agent logs.

## Required Sections

- completed in the last 24 hours
- in progress today
- blockers or failures

## Rules

- keep summaries factual
- call out failed workflows clearly
- do not invent progress that is not supported by logs or commits
